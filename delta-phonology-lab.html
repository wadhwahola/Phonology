This trainer uses a broad phonemic model of Standard Southern British English (the accent usually labelled RP or GB in ELT materials), following the Gimson/Cruttenden inventory used by Cambridge dictionaries and by most DELTA reference books. Real speakers and dictionaries show legitimate variation; where a form is contested the item says so. Device text-to-speech produces careful citation forms and will not reliably demonstrate assimilation, elision or weak forms — treat the printed IPA, not the audio, as the model. The exam-craft modules draw on published Cambridge Delta Module One examination reports and on the task weightings given by ELT Concourse and Teflpedia; the practice items are written for this trainer and are not Cambridge material. Check the current Handbook for Tutors and Candidates before relying on any mark or timing figure.
